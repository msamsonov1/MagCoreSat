Electromagnetic & Thermodynamic Governing Formulations

Reduced-order physical models implemented inside the real-time Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) to track high-temperature permeability roll-off, modified Steinmetz dissipation, and multiphase flux balancing in vacuum.

1. Temperature-Dependent Flux Saturation (MnZn Ferrite)

In high-frequency MnZn power ferrites (e.g., 3C95 / N87 class used in compact 100–500 kHz planar transformers), spontaneous magnetization collapses sharply as core temperature approaches the Curie point ($T_c \approx 220^\circ\text{C}$):

$$B_{sat}(T) = B_{0}\left(1 - \frac{T + 273.15}{T_c + 273.15}\right)^\beta$$

With $B_0 \approx 0.64\,\text{T}$ and critical exponent $\beta \approx 0.42$, $B_{sat}$ drops from $0.52\,\text{T}$ at $-50^\circ\text{C}$ to $0.28\,\text{T}$ at $145^\circ\text{C}$ (~46% reduction). Static designs sized at room temperature saturate immediately at lunar noon.

2. Thermal Steinmetz Core Dissipation

Volumetric core loss $P_v$ scales non-linearly with flux swing $\Delta B$, switching frequency $f_{sw}$, and temperature. Above the ferrite loss minimum (~$80^\circ\text{C}–95^\circ\text{C}$), positive thermal feedback accelerates heating:

$$P_v(T, \Delta B) = k \cdot f_{sw}^\alpha \cdot \left(\frac{\Delta B}{2}\right)^\gamma \cdot \left(c_0 - c_1 T + c_2 T^2\right)$$

In lunar vacuum, there is zero convective cooling. If core loss rises faster than baseplate conduction can reject heat, $T_{core}$ climbs toward $T_c$, collapsing $B_{sat}(T)$ and triggering hard-switching shoot-through.

3. Active Interleave Flux & Thermal Balancing

When the observer detects diminishing margin $M_k(t) = B_{sat}(T_k) - B_{pk,k}(t)$ or leg-to-leg thermal drift, the controller trims per-leg duty $D_k$ and phase angle $\phi_k$ across $N$ parallel legs (plus N+1 hot-spare rotation under extreme zenith heat):

$$B_{pk,k} = \frac{V_{in} \cdot \left(D_k + \Delta D_k\right)}{2 N_{turn} A_c f_{sw}} + B_{dc,k}(\Delta \phi_k)$$

Eliminating volt-second imbalance ($B_{dc,k} \to 0$) and shifting current away from thermally stressed legs prevents single-leg saturation without requiring 2×–3× oversized magnetic core mass.

4. Lunar Surface Vacuum Heat Rejection

Because lunar regolith is an extreme thermal insulator ($k_{reg} \approx 0.002\,\text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$), planar transformer losses must conduct into a coldplate and reject via zenith-facing radiators:

$$C_{th} \frac{dT_{core}}{dt} = P_{core}(B, T) + P_{cu}(I) - \frac{T_{core} - T_{rad}}{R_{cond}} - \varepsilon \sigma_{SB} A_{rad} \left(T_{rad}^4 - T_{sink}^4\right)$$

By safely operating magnetics closer to their thermal ceiling with guaranteed flux-margin protection, MagCoreSat reduces radiator area and core mass penalties for lunar surface PMAD substations.

Dual-Use Commercialization Path

Terrestrial High-Density Power Electronics Pull

The same firmware IP that protects lunar planar transformers against midday thermal saturation directly addresses high-temperature derating in commercial wide-bandgap (SiC/GaN) power converters.

1. 800V AI Data Center Power Supply Units (GaN Planar)

Thermal Bottleneck: High-density 800V-to-48V server rack converters run planar cores above $105^\circ\text{C}$. Sudden GPU compute load steps can push hot cores across $B_{sat}(T)$, causing switch failure.

MagCoreSat Advantage: Firmware-only flux headroom guard enables full-power transient ride-through without adding bulky heatsinks or derating rack density.

2. Megawatt EV Fast Chargers (350 kW–1 MW DCFC)

Thermal Bottleneck: In $45^\circ\text{C}+$ desert ambient conditions, internal transformer temperatures exceed $115^\circ\text{C}$, reducing saturation margin by >25% during continuous fleet charging.

MagCoreSat Advantage: Active multi-leg flux balancing prevents saturation trips and reduces liquid-cooling mass per charger cabinet.

3. Commercial LEO/GEO Satellite Power Conditioning

Thermal Bottleneck: Rapid eclipse-to-sunlight thermal gradients impose severe $\Delta T$ swings on spacecraft EPS DC-DC converters, traditionally forcing 2×–3× magnetic core mass margins.

MagCoreSat Advantage: Real-time $(B, T)$ state estimation trims transformer mass and volume while preserving single-fault tolerance.

4. Utility Solar & Grid-Forming BESS Inverters

Thermal Bottleneck: Peak solar generation coincides with peak afternoon cabinet temperatures, forcing thermal power curtailment to protect magnetic filter stages.

MagCoreSat Advantage: Dynamic phase/duty balancing extends peak-temperature operating envelopes without hardware redesign.
